WordDrop Privacy Policy

Effective 17 September 2026 · Package com.avirana.worddrop

WordDrop is a free, offline vocabulary app for Android. This policy explains what the app does — and does not — do with your information.

What we collect

Nothing. WordDrop does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

  • The app has no internet permission. It cannot send data anywhere.
  • There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no crash-reporting SDKs and no advertising. It does not use the Android advertising ID.
  • The word bank is bundled inside the app. No dictionary lookups leave your device.

What stays on your device

WordDrop stores a small amount of data locally so it can work:

  • Which words you have seen, saved, and how you did on quizzes.
  • Your settings (widget refresh interval, difficulty, categories).

This data lives only in the app's private storage on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Backups

If you have Android Backup enabled on your device, Android may include this local data in your Google account backup so it can be restored on a new phone. That backup is managed by Android and Google under Google's Privacy Policy, not by WordDrop. You can turn off backups in your device's system settings.

Permissions

WordDrop requests no runtime permissions.

Children

WordDrop does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If a future version of the app starts collecting data (for example, optional cloud sync), this policy will be updated and the change will be noted in the app's release notes before it takes effect.
